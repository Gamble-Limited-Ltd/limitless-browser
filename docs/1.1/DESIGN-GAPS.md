Step 1 of the 1.1 design pass (`docs/1.1/PLAN.md` scope 1). Audit date 2026-11-16, base `release/1.1.0` at `883fa23`.
Truth for what to build: Boss's picks in `docs/1.0/design/picks-round3.md` (2026-11-14). The design itself is the live Design Room III
artifact (copy `docs/1.0/design/design-room-3.html` is the earlier snapshot; this audit read the live HTML of 2026-11-16, 1365 lines, rendered and
screenshotted in Chromium with the design's Google Fonts blocked, so serif/script faces in the screenshots were fallbacks; the CSS tokens below were read from source).
This doc changes no app code. Boss's Nos are skipped: Silver plate, Onyx and gold certificate, Oxide theme, Champagne gold, Next steps one line, The seal is pressed.
Rules that hold for every package: tokens only (no hard-coded hex in components), `textContent` for dynamic text, no em dashes in copy, straight quotes,
motion 160 to 240 ms with `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`, reduced motion collapses to a 150 ms fade or none, 4.5:1 text contrast in every theme (`docs/1.0/UI.md` 1).
