The plan in one screen

Four steps up. Ordinal is free and uses shared public relays. Cardinal and Prime run on our own relay. Limitless Access is invite-only: only people I grant it to can even see it. All limits and prices are placeholders that change once the relay is measured.

Fixed rules behind every design below

  • One purchase is one year, counted from the day I grant it. A plan that ends drops back to Ordinal, with notices 14 days and 3 days before.
  • Cash, by hand. You ask on the page, I reply, we meet, and I set the paid-until date.
  • Refund: a 24 hour trial first, then a full refund within 3 days, then none unless we end the service.
  • Certificates by tier: none for Ordinal, a nice one for Cardinal, a really nice one for Prime, the best for Access. The QR code appears only on Prime and Access.

The QR code

What the QR code does

It is a proof of authenticity, nothing else. Scanning it opens the verify page for that certificate's code (like LMT-007-7K4Q). The page says genuine, superseded (a newer certificate replaced it) or void (the membership ended), plus the number, the tier and the date admitted. It never shows the name. It is not a sign-in and unlocks nothing.

Only Prime and Access certificates carry it, because those are the ones members are allowed to show to others. It is small and sits clear of the text and the seal.

  • Free runs on shared public relays. Speed varies and can slow at busy times.
  • Public relays can go down, and we do not promise they will not.
  • The operator of a public relay can see which sites your connection reaches, not what you type.
