# Limitless 1.0: Membership Charter and Rules (draft)

Status: **draft, decisions complete** (two question rounds with Boss, 2026-11-10). Boss pre-approved Claude's wording for
this release ("auto approve your work, when work begins"). The text below is final in substance; the wording gets one
polish pass in S5 and is then versioned by date (`2026-MM-DD`). A new version makes every member sign again (ceremony
step 3 only).

## 1. Decisions
- **Two documents:** a short **Membership Charter** (numbered Articles, "The Member shall", signed in the ceremony,
  printed on certificate page 3) and the full **Membership Rules** (definitions, severity table, enforcement, appeals),
  served ungated at `/legal/membership.html` next to Terms and Privacy and linked from HALL's legal pages.
- **Confidentiality covers:** the address and invite links, the member list and numbers, screenshots and recordings of the
  app, and certificates (only Members who hold one, that is Cardinal, Prime and Executive members, and the Director may show one).
- **Allowed:** saying you are a member; talking freely with other members; telling a **parent or guardian** anything;
  answering **school staff who ask** honestly; any disclosure **required by law**. The open-source code and its licence
  are never covered (AGPL).
- **Severity** (section 3): serious = account sharing or a second account, selling or trading access, exploiting a
  security problem or deliberate relay abuse. Moderate = publishing the address or an invite link. Minor = small or
  accidental misuse (for example one heavy download that hits the cap): "minor abuse, not exploitation, is more or less
  only a warning" (Boss).
- **Steps:** warning, suspension (length set by the Director each time: 7, 14, 30 days or indefinite), removal. Serious
  breaches may skip straight to removal.
- **During a suspension** the member sees the suspension letter and a read-only view of their membership page
  (certificate, standing, charter); no browsing.
- **Removal notice names the Article** that was broken. Certificate void, number retired.
- **Appeals:** one written appeal per decision, by email to gamblelimitedsupport@taps-holdings.org with "APPEAL" at the
  start of the subject, within 14 days. The Director's decision on the appeal is final.
- **Prime immunity: REMOVED** (Boss, 2026-11-13, applied 2026-11-14). Prime is sold, so no tier has moderation immunity.
- **Under 18:** no extra wording (members are 13 or older, the GLL ID rule).
- **Governing law and venue:** the same clause as the HALL legal v2 Terms, referenced, so the documents never disagree.
- **Acceptance:** typed full legal name, tick, Sign, stored with the version and time.

## 2. Charter (draft text)
**Article I. Membership.** Membership of Limitless Browser is personal and by invitation. The Member shall hold one
membership only.

**Article II. Confidentiality.** The Member shall not disclose the address of Limitless Browser, any invitation, or the
identity or number of any other Member, to any person who is not a Member, and shall not publish screenshots or
recordings of Limitless Browser. The Member may say that they are a Member, may speak freely with other Members, may tell
a parent or guardian, may answer school staff who ask, and may make any disclosure the law requires. The open-source
code of Limitless Browser and its licence are not covered by this Article.

**Article III. The account.** The Member shall not share their sign-in or session with anyone, shall not hold a second
account, and shall not sell or trade any invitation, number or account.

**Article IV. Use.** The Member shall obey the law and remains bound by the rules of their own school and network. The
Member shall not attack, scan or overload the relay, and shall not attempt to bypass its limits.

**Article V. Security.** The Member shall report any security problem privately to the Director and shall not exploit it.

**Article VI. Reserved.** (Nominations were removed on 2026-11-14. The number is kept so later Articles do not move.)

**Article VII. Certificates.** A certificate is issued from the Cardinal plan upward and is personal. Only a Member who holds a certificate, and the Director, may show it.

**Article VIII. Enforcement.** A breach may lead to a warning, a suspension or removal, as set out in the Membership Rules.
A Member may appeal a decision once, in writing, within fourteen days.

## 3. Severity table (for the full Rules)
| Level | Examples | Usual outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | One heavy download or a cap hit; an accidental breach noticed and stopped by the member; a screenshot shared privately with one person | Warning |
| Moderate | Posting the address or an invite link (group chat, social media); a second minor breach after a warning | Suspension (length chosen each time); removal if repeated |
| Serious | Sharing a sign-in or session; a second account; selling or trading invitations, numbers or accounts; deliberate attacks, scanning or cap bypass; exploiting a security problem | Removal possible at once |
The same table applies to every plan. A paid plan changes what happens to the money, not how a breach is judged (section 6).
The Director may treat a case as more or less serious than the table when the facts call for it; the notice says which
Article applied.

## 6. Rules clause 8: Plans and payments (draft text, 2026-11-14)
Source: `PAID-TIERS-PLAN.md` 10c. Items in [brackets] still need Boss's word. Plain words, no em dashes.

**8.1 Plans.** Ordinal is free. Cardinal and Prime are paid plans bought for one term. Further plans are granted by
invitation only, by the Director. The plans page shows what each public plan includes as typical limits. A limit is
not a guarantee.

**8.2 Who may buy.** A Member who has signed the Charter may ask to buy a plan. A Member under 18 ticks a box to
confirm they are allowed to buy; the Director does not check further. The Director may decline a request without giving
a reason. Nobody can ask for an invitation-only plan: only the Director invites.

**8.3 How buying works.** The Member sends a request. The Director replies and the Member pays in cash, by hand. The
Director records the payment and grants the plan. The term is 12 months from the day the plan is granted. No card or
bank details are asked for or kept.

**8.4 Provider and receipt.** The provider is Gamble Limited Ltd. Each payment gives a receipt in the Member's account
panel: date, amount, plan, paid-until date and how it was paid, with a snapshot of the plan's price and limits at the
time of purchase. The snapshot is what was promised for that term.

**8.5 Trials.** Once a month, each account that is at least 7 days old and verified may start a free 24 hour trial of
the plan one step up (Ordinal tries Cardinal, Cardinal tries Prime). Nothing is charged when a trial ends.

**8.6 What we promise.** We run the relay on a best-effort basis. We do not guarantee speed, availability or uptime.
If our relay is unavailable for more than 24 hours in a row, as shown on the Status page, each full day adds one day
to the paid-until date. Shorter outages earn no credit.

**8.7 Fallback.** When a data limit is reached or our relay is down, browsing continues on third-party public relays.
The Member sees a notice the first time each fallback starts. The operators of those relays can see connection
information (see the Privacy policy).

**8.8 Changes to limits.** We may change a plan's limits with at least 14 days' notice in the app and the account
panel. If a change makes a Member's plan clearly worse than their purchase snapshot, they may ask for a refund of the
unused months, worked out pro rata. [Time to ask: 30 days from the day the change starts.]

**8.9 Refunds.** A full refund is available within 3 days of the day the plan is granted. After that there is no
refund, except: (a) if we end the service, a pro rata refund of the unused months; (b) a real cut under 8.8; (c) an
error, a duplicate payment or a late request that was not the Member's fault, where the Director decides the pro rata
amount each time. Extra data packs (top-ups) are never refunded. A Member who leaves or deletes their account after the
first 3 days gets no refund. Refunds are paid the same way the Member paid, in cash by hand, and are logged.

**8.10 Ended plans.** A plan ends with no refund if the Member is removed for breaking the Rules, sells, shares or
trades the account, abuses the relay (heavy or automated traffic) or uses it for anything illegal or for attacks. The
notice names the Article broken. The Member has one appeal (section 9 of these Rules). If the appeal succeeds the plan
is restored and the paid-until date moves forward by the days lost. A suspension or mute does not stop the paid-until
date running.

**8.11 Relay abuse.** Hours of sustained top-speed use, automated traffic, a shared account or illegal use cause the
Member's speed to be lowered automatically. The Director is alerted and then restores the plan or ends it under 8.10.

**8.12 Renewal, upgrade, expiry.** Renewing early adds 12 months to the current end date, up to 2 years ahead. Moving
up a plan costs the pro rata difference. When a plan expires the Member returns to Ordinal. Notices are sent 14 days and
3 days before. The membership number stays with the Member. The certificate stays as a keepsake, and its verify page
says "genuine, lapsed" until the plan is renewed.

**8.13 Extra data packs.** Packs last until the plan's yearly expiry and carry over if the plan is renewed. The Director
records each pack by hand. [Sizes and prices are set after measuring.]

**8.14 Not transferable.** A plan belongs to one Member and cannot be given, sold or shared. A friend may buy their own,
or the Director may grant a plan to a named Member and record it as an ordinary grant.

**8.15 Membership numbers.** Ordinal and Cardinal Members choose a number that is not prime. A Member moving up to Prime
chooses a free prime number, and their old number is retired. Members on invitation-only plans choose from their own
range or ask the Director. A prime number stays with its holder for life, even if the plan lapses.

**8.16 Records.** Payment records are kept for 7 years. The reason a Member wrote on an access request is deleted once it
is decided. The Privacy policy has the rest.

**8.17 Support.** Questions from paying Members are answered first. We aim to reply within 3 days. This is an aim, not a
promise.

**8.18 The law.** Nothing in these Rules limits rights the law gives you. Governing law and venue are the same as the
Terms.
