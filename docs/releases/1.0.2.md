## What changed
- **Our relay showed "unreachable" in Settings > Connection:** with third-party relays allowed, the pool benchmark (`probeWispPoolServer`) opened plain sockets to the same-origin `/wisp/`, which answers 410 since the relay moved to the VPS. Our relay was then listed as unreachable and ranked last, although sockets to the VPS worked. The benchmark now probes our entry the way the startup probe does: a relay token, then the VPS URL with `lb.v1` and `lbt.<token>`. No token (plan without relay, VPS down) still counts as unhealthy.
- Test hook `window.LB_probePool`; test in `tests/e2e/relaytoken.spec.js`.
