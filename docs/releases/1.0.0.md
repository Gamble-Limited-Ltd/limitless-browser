- Our relay is back, on the VPS (`relay.taps-holdings.org`, DNS only, not through Cloudflare): short-lived signed tokens from home lbgate, fair share by plan, byte counts synced home every second. Public relays stay the fallback. Build defaults `LB_FEATURE_OWNRELAY=true`, `LB_FEATURE_OWNSOCKETS=4`.
- Membership: numbers, charter, welcome ceremony, certificates, standing (suspend, remove), public verify status (no names). Legal names are stored encrypted with `MEMBER_DATA_KEY`.
- Every GLL ID gets the free Ordinal tier (GLL ID client `limitless-browser` switched to open; sign-in stays required).
- New UI: Limitless menu, settings groups, 13 themes (5 paid), calm error cards, address bar domain view, vertical tabs, tab cap for Ordinal.
- Paid tiers engine: plans page, account panel, request form (queues a copy to the support inbox), trials, top-ups, Load Now, transfer brake. `plans_open` ships OFF.
- Downloads panel; Report a problem; admin queue and load-time chart.

## Known issues
- **Per-stream relay speed (VPS test 2026-11-15):** a single stream through the relay gets about 2 to 2.5 Mbit/s while the VPS link does 200+ Mbit/s on one plain connection. Pages use many streams and are less affected; one large download or one video stream is. Cause not found yet (WISP flow control or the gate's backpressure are the suspects). Plan speeds above about 10 Mbit/s cannot be reached by a single member until this is fixed.
- The VPS test's cleanup command in S4-HOST-COMMANDS 9.1 was `down`, which also stopped the real VPS stack for about 2 minutes on 2026-11-15 (no members on it yet). Fixed to `rm -sf loadtest relaygate-lt`.
- Transfer: RackNerd meters in plus out and every relayed byte crosses twice, so 5 TB is about 2.5 TB delivered to members.
- Hallmark and Impeccable design passes were not run on the S6 screens; the Charter step has an empty band.
- e2e takes about 15 minutes (was 3.6). Page speed itself passed.
- `patcher.html` still carries tutorial patch definitions that match nothing.
- Prices are placeholders; "Plans open for sale" ships OFF until Boss's legal self-check.
- HALL verify shares one gate rate bucket (R3 above).
