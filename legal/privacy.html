Summary

  • Limitless Browser is invite only. You sign in with GLL ID, and we keep only what we need to know who you are and to stop abuse.
  • We do not keep logs of the sites you visit or of what you send and receive, and we do not tie any such record to your account.
  • The relay that carries your traffic can see which host and port you connect to. It cannot read the content of encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Your tabs, cookies jar and settings stay in your browser's storage on your device.
  • Third-party fallback relays are off by default, are not run by Gamble Limited Ltd., and are not covered by this policy.
  • We do not sell data. We have nothing worth selling and no wish to start.

This summary helps you read the policy. Only the formal text below counts.

§ 1: The Service

Limitless Browser (the "Service") is a private browsing proxy at limitless.taps-holdings.org. It is run by Gamble Limited Ltd. ("we", "us"). "Gamble Limited Ltd." is the name under which this project operates; the Terms of Use of the company website explain what that means. This policy covers the Service only. It does not cover the company website, any game, GLL ID itself, or any website you visit through the Service.

§ 2: Who Can Use the Service

The Service is invite only. To use it you must sign in through GLL ID, and the account must have been approved for Limitless Browser. If you are not approved, you see a page that says the Service is invite only, and you are not sent the app or the relay. Only the legal pages, the theme files (styles, fonts and logos) and the icon are served without a sign-in.

§ 4: How We Use the Data

We use the data in § 3 to check who may use the Service, to run it, to apply limits, to keep it safe from abuse, and to answer questions you send us. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

The operator has an admin panel that shows, for each account, its identifier, email address, last sign-in, usage counters and any limits, and lets the operator grant or withdraw access, change limits or cut off an account. Each action in the panel is recorded with the account it concerned, for 90 days.

§ 5: The Relay

The Service reaches websites through a relay that Gamble Limited Ltd. runs. Your browser opens a few connections to the relay (up to four), and the relay makes the real connections to the sites you visit. To do that, the relay sees the host name or address and the port of each connection you ask for. For HTTPS sites, the relay carries encrypted traffic and cannot read it. Anything that is not encrypted, such as a plain HTTP site, can be read by anyone along the path, including the relay.

The relay refuses connections to private and internal addresses and to some ports (mail, IRC and common file-sharing ports). It does not write a routine record of what it connects to. See § 3 for the abuse events we keep.

§ 6: Third-Party Fallback Relays

If our relay cannot be reached, the app can offer third-party relays instead. They are off by default and you must turn them on yourself, on each device. Gamble Limited Ltd. does not run them. Their operators can see what the relay sees (see § 5), and our limits, logging rules and this policy do not cover them. You can turn them off again in settings. If you also turn on the optional setting that uses several third-party relays at once, different sites, or different connections to the same site, can go through different operators, and in Race mode the start of a secure connection is sent to two of them. Each of them can see which sites you visit.

§ 7: Data in Your Browser

The browser part of the Service keeps your tabs, cookies jar, settings and similar data in your browser's own storage on your device. It is not sent to us. It is lost if you clear site data, use a private window or change device.

The Service also keeps a cache of page files (styles, scripts, images and pages that allow it) in your browser's storage, up to 200 MB, so repeat visits load faster. It stays on your device and is not sent to us. Files that a site marks as private or not to be stored are not kept. You can turn the cache off, or clear it, in settings.

If sync or other storage features are added later, this policy will be updated before they start.

§ 8: GLL ID and Cloudflare

Sign-in is handled by GLL ID (id.taps-holdings.org), another service of Gamble Limited Ltd. Its own documents describe what it holds about your account. The Service receives only what it needs (your account identifier and email address, and whether you are approved).

Traffic to the Service passes through Cloudflare, which ends the encrypted connection on our behalf and processes your IP address and request details under its own terms and privacy policy.

§ 9: Cookies

The Service sets one session cookie after you sign in. It is limited to this site, is not readable by page scripts, is sent only over secure connections, and lasts 7 days. It is not used for tracking or advertising. Cloudflare may set a cookie that is strictly necessary for security under its own policy.

§ 10: Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with the services needed to run the Service (Cloudflare, and GLL ID for sign-in), and when the law requires it. We disclose data in answer to a legal request only when the request is valid and binding on us, and then only the data in § 3 that we still hold at that time.

§ 11: Your Rights

You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to fix it, ask us to delete it, or object to our use of it. Write to gamblelimitedsupport@taps-holdings.org with "PRIVACY" at the start of the subject line. We answer within thirty (30) days. We may need to check that the request comes from the account holder. Data held by Cloudflare, by GLL ID, by a fallback relay operator or by a website you visit is outside this policy; contact that party.

§ 12: Children

The Service is for people who are 13 years old or older, the same age that GLL ID requires for an account. We do not knowingly give access to a person under 13. If you believe that a person under 13 has access, contact us (see § 15) and we will withdraw the access and delete the data in § 3 about that account.

§ 13: Changes

We may change this policy. We will change the effective date at the top. If we add a feature that collects new data, we will update this policy before the feature starts.
